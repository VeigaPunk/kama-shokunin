## 4. Findings

- **F1 — control-suite coverage gap (the deliverable).** 19/27 counter mutants survive every committed control at the met level. Root pattern: every committed negative control fails *several* legs at once — `junk-c2-allunverifiable` fails live **and** hygiene (its receipt: `live=0 hygiene=0`), `junk-c3-rowsonly`/`stagedverbatim` fail 3–4 legs — so single-leg weakenings are invisible. Sharpest instances: (a) **hygiene** — no control isolates a temp-leak with all other legs passing, so the routed R41A10-10 defect class (round-41 counters leaking temp exports) could regress invisibly (M02/M14 demonstrated at P-M02); (b) **`external_ok>=4`** — `junk-c2-allunverifiable` models exactly this defect, but its coincident hygiene leak masks the met flip, so M01 survives (demonstrated at P-M01). Proposed fix (draft, not applied): land two one-leg-away C2 controls — allunverifiable-with-clean-r41-counters and sep-c2-only-with-leaky-r41-counters (both recipes are in `R42D07/probes.py`, ~10 lines each) — and for C3, single-leg controls per gate/census/rows/coverage sub-check (the P-N* recipes).
- **F2 — `sep-c2-only.c2.out` composition not reproducible.** The committed receipt's `live-reference: tokens=94 blob=85 external_ok=5 … unverifiable=3 …`; my recipe-faithful reconstruction reproduces verdict and all five legs byte-exact but `blob=84 external_ok=4 unverifiable=5`. No committed ledger revision has 5 external rows (all four — `dd9f5dc`, `2793eb5`, `c8c92db`, `3d9bd8d` — carry 4 external + 5 unverifiable; verified by `git show <rev>:registry/digest-ledger.tsv | grep -c`). [INFERENCE] the receipt was generated against an intermediate ledger state during the C2 claim work, before the committed one. Minor: the control's discriminating power (C2 files alone → met=True) is unaffected; an errata line in the controls dir would make the receipt regenerable.
- **F3 — positive result: the C2 cross-check is the strong defense.** The tool-vs-reference byte-equality plus the 8 seeded randomized contract cases kill all six classify-level mutations (M04, M06, M07, M08, M09, M12) at every real-tool state, and the positive control R-SEPC2 kills M12 at the met level. Keep both properties when extending the counter.
- **F4 — robustness notes.** N02's base-absence check is subsumed by the base-ground scan (the entry itself carries the ground strings) — redundant but harmless. N04's guard order (digest computed before the evidence-absence check) turns the minimal mutation into a crash rather than an acceptance — the current order is the safer one by accident; if the rows leg is ever refactored, keep the digest computation after the guard or the crash becomes a silent accept.
