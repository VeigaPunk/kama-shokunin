## 1. The proposal being tested

`considerthese/2026-10-25.md:27` (read raw), §Self-improvement suggestions, item 1:

> **Recompute every digest and S-id that a new lesson or row cites:** the S472 digest (right prefix,
> wrong body) and round 36's five misattributed ids are exactly the kind of thing a read-only daily
> pass can check mechanically. I could hash each committed evidence file a new row points at and compare
> full digests, and cross-check every S-id named in lesson prose against the row it is said to be, then
> report mismatches the morning after they land instead of one round later. This differs from re-opening
> quotes (suggested 10-24) because it checks identifiers and hashes, not wording.

Proposal 1 therefore names **three** separable obligations:

| # | Obligation | Where it must live |
|---|---|---|
| P1-a | hash each committed evidence file a new row points at and compare full digests | a tool that recomputes |
| P1-b | cross-check every S-id named in lesson prose against the row it is said to be | a tool that resolves ids |
| P1-c | report mismatches **the morning after they land**, instead of one round later | a recurring (daily) driver |

`preregistration.json` claims C1 "also implements operator `considerthese/2026-10-25.md` proposal 1
(recompute every digest and S-id a new lesson or row cites)". The frozen predicate is the three-leg
counter `tools/round40-calibration-audit.py`.

## 2. Verdict

**C1 does NOT implement proposal 1 as written.** It implements a *one-shot, one-time* correction of
two already-known r38-routed digest defects, gated by string-presence checks against hardcoded
constants. It covers neither the recomputation mechanism (P1-a), nor the S-id cross-check (P1-b), nor
the morning-after recurrence (P1-c). The claim "C1 also implements proposal 1" is an overclaim; the
lesson's Assimilation block must not state it as implemented.

Concretely, across the whole lane at tip `efe25f96`:

- **No tool recomputes a cited digest.** `tools/round40-calibration-audit.py:23` imports `hashlib`
  and `json`, and **uses neither** — AST-verified: `UNUSED: ['hashlib', 'json']` (the only textual
  occurrence of each is the import line itself). The counter compares three hardcoded 64-hex
  *string constants* against blob text. It never opens, hashes, or even names the evidence artefact
  `.ufo/waves/round-38/assimilation/exec-tag-inventory/ls-remote-tags.txt` — grep for
  `ls-remote-tags|exec-tag-inventory|round-38/assimilation` in both round-40 tools returns nothing.
  `CORRECT_EVIDENCE` / `CORRECT_PACKAGE` are literals (`tools/round40-calibration-audit.py:26-28`),
  not computed values.
- **No tool cross-checks an S-id named in lesson prose against the row it is said to be.** The
  round-40 counter never resolves an S-id to its row. `tools/citation-provenance.py` verifies
  `cite(rev, path, sha256=…)` tokens, but its registry `registry/required-citations.json` holds 17
  rows, all round-18 artefacts, and `corpus/SOURCES.md` contains **zero** `cite(` tokens — the
  registry row format carries no digest-bearing citation token at all. `tools/round38-statement-audit.py:67`
  (`staged_rows_ok`) checks that five *staged* lines carry five ids, but it reads the staged working
  tree, not lesson prose. The nearest thing to a prose→row resolver is `staged_rows_ok`, which is
  staged-file-scoped, not prose-scoped, and is not invoked by either round-40 counter.
- **No recurrence driver exists.** grep for `daily|morning|cron|recurring` across `tools/*.py` returns
  nothing. `considerthese/` is referenced only by wave-spec generators (`.ufo/waves/round-{16,36,37}/*/make-spec.py`),
  never by a checker. The r38 defect was found one round late by an outside closure — exactly the
  latency proposal 1 asks to remove — and C1 does not shorten it.

The one thing C1 *does* do is discharge the two specific r38-routed corrections (defects 1 and 2 of
`corpus/lessons/round-38-verifier-spotcheck.md:73-74`). That is a genuine calibration improvement, but
it is the *correction*, not the *practice* proposal 1 asked for.

### 4.3 D1 — the `teaches`/`status` column divergence (confirmatory, already reported by siblings)

The frozen predicate sentence says the S474 **`teaches`** cell must start `supersedes S472`. The
`corpus/SOURCES.md` header is `| id | source | kind | tier | scope | anchor | teaches | status | opened |
recheck | used-by |` (line 11), so a parsed row holds `teaches` at index **6** and `status` at index
**7**. The counter reads `row[7]` at `tools/round40-calibration-audit.py:63` and `:81`.

Fixture, same bytes, marker placed in each column:

| fixture | marker location | `s474_row` | `met` |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | `teaches` (index 6) — **predicate-conforming** | `0` | `False` |
| B | `status` (index 7) — schema-violating | `1` | `True` |

So the frozen counter cannot credit a schema-valid S474 row; only a row that writes the correction
into the `status` column (whose observed domain is `mined`/`queued`/`rejected`) is credited. Every
existing superseding row in the corpus puts the marker in `teaches` (verified at `corpus/SOURCES.md`
lines 43, 52, 59 — `S031`, `S040`, `S056`). Independently reported by R40A04, R40A07, R40E06, R40E13;
recorded here because it is the mechanism that makes the proposal-1 gap bite, and because the
`false_corrections` leg has the same defect (D2).

### 4.4 D2 — `false_corrections` misses a second bogus superseding row (same off-by-one)

`tools/round40-calibration-audit.py:80-82` scans for rows other than S474 whose `row[7]` starts
`supersedes S472`. Because `row[7]` is `status`, a second bogus superseding row written the
schema-valid way (marker in `teaches`) is **not counted**.

Fixture: S474 (marker in `teaches`) **plus** a bogus S475 also carrying `supersedes S472` in its
`teaches` cell → `s474_row=0 amend_corrected=1 r36d2c_fixed=1 false_corrections=0 met=False`.

The counter reports `false_corrections=0` for a registry containing a second false correction. The
predicate's `false_corrections == 0` guard is therefore inert against the exact defect class it exists
to catch. This is a **distinct** hole from D1 and was not reported by any sibling: no file in
`.ufo/waves/round-40/w1/out/` mentions a second superseding row (grep for `S475` across 39 sibling
reports: no hit).

### 4.5 D3 — the wrong-digest-outside-the-section leg is dead on the landed shape

`tools/round40-calibration-audit.py:84` reads `if WRONG_EVIDENCE in amend and not amend_corrected:`.
Once the amendment carries a valid `## Correction — round 40` section (which is exactly the shape C1
requires, and which necessarily contains the wrong digest), `amend_corrected` is `1`, the guard
`not amend_corrected` is `False`, and the wrong digest sitting elsewhere in the file is never counted.
The comment on line 83 — "Wrong full digest anywhere outside the correction sections counts as false" —
describes behaviour the code does not have.

Fixture, mirroring the real landed shape (immutable `source:` line still carrying the wrong digest,
correction section appended at the end): `s474_row=0 amend_corrected=1 r36d2c_fixed=1
false_corrections=0 met=False` — `false_corrections=0` despite the wrong digest being present
**outside** the correction section.

R40SP01 and R40S05 both note the `and not amend_corrected` clause in prose; the executable
demonstration that it neutralises the guard in the landed shape is what I add here. This matters for
the lesson: the correction's *append-only* landing leaves the stale digest in the amendment's `source:`
line by design, so the guard can never fire on the shape C1 actually produces — `false_corrections == 0`
is true for reasons unrelated to the absence of false corrections.

**D3b — the leg is a one-shot ratchet, dead for every future round (new).** Because the guard reads
`not amend_corrected`, the *only* state in which the wrong-digest clause can fire is the pre-landing
state. The instant a `## Correction — round 40` section exists, the clause is unreachable forever —
the section's own required content contains `WRONG_EVIDENCE`, so `amend_corrected` can never again be
`0` for this amendment.

Fixture (a **future** defect, not the r40 one): the landed r40 shape *plus* a new
`## Correction — round 41` section carrying a fresh wrong digest
`9999999999999999999999999999999999999999999999999999999999999999` outside the r40 section →
`s474_row=0 amend_corrected=1 r36d2c_fixed=1 false_corrections=0 met=False`. A newly-introduced wrong
digest is invisible to the guard.

This also explains the base-control prose discrepancy flagged for the L1 by R40S09 (and independently by
R40A04 §2 and R40S08 §2): `preregistration.json` C1 `kill_rule` asserts "At base (efe25f96) every leg
is 0 and false_corrections == 0", but the authoritative counter at base and the committed control
`.ufo/waves/round-40/controls/c1-base.out` both read `false_corrections=1`. The `1` comes from exactly
this clause — the amendment blob carries the wrong digest and `amend_corrected` is `0` at base. So the
preregistration prose is wrong about the base value, **and** the only way the landing drives that `1`
to `0` is by landing the section that permanently disables the clause. The `false_corrections == 0`
predicate leg is therefore satisfied by construction of the defect's own fix, not by the absence of
false corrections. Non-blocking for the kill rule (met is False at base either way), but it should be
disclosed in the lesson's counter-compat notes.

### 4.6 D4 — the lesson leg is token presence, not a correction row

`tools/round40-calibration-audit.py:76` reads `if CORRECT_PACKAGE in lesson and "R36-D2C" in lesson:`.
The predicate sentence requires that `corpus/lessons/round-40.md` "carries a correction row naming

Fixture: a lesson with **no correction row at all**, whose prose merely mentions the two tokens
("Earlier we saw R36-D2C mention and the digest … in passing. Nothing corrected here.") →
`r36d2c_fixed=1`. The leg cannot distinguish a landed correction row from an incidental mention.

## 6. S-id cross-check — the concrete gap proposal 1 names

Proposal 1 names round 36's five misattributed ids (S332, S336, S337, S338, S339) as the worked
example. Facts established:

- The five ids are **correct** in `corpus/lessons/round-38.md:51`: at
  `/home/vgpnk/Projects/shokuknow/merge/kama/SOURCES.append.md` lines 9, 13, 14, 15, 16 the rows are
  exactly `S332`, `S336`, `S337`, `S338`, `S339` (verified by reading those lines).
- The mechanism that verified this is `tools/round38-statement-audit.py:67` `staged_rows_ok()` — a
  **staged-file** line→id check. It is not a lesson-prose→registry-row cross-check, and it reads the
  staged working tree rather than a pinned blob.
- Round 36's defect (a wrong four-id *range* in prose, `corpus/lessons/round-36.md:43`) was of
  exactly the prose→row kind. A prose→row resolver would have caught it. No such resolver exists in
  the lane at `efe25f96`.

Recommendation for the lesson's deferral note: the S-id cross-check is the half of proposal 1 that
would have prevented the r36-D2 defect class, and it is the half with **no** instrument at all —
strongest candidate for a future round. A minimal form: extract every `S\d{3,}` token from the
committed lesson prose and assert each resolves to a row in `corpus/SOURCES.md` at the same tip, and
that ids asserted to come from the staged package resolve to the staged rows.
