**F2 — the round-47 tracker missed a sibling seat in its own wave, and its method cannot see seat-level uptake at all.** `.ufo/waves/round-47/w1/out/S8Proposals/table.md:30` records row 20 as "no round", although seat `U2Pins` in the same build spec (`.ufo/waves/round-47/w1/build_spec.py:113-114`) had already produced the pin gap (`.ufo/waves/round-47/w1/out/U2Pins/pins.md:16`) 10 minutes earlier (`pins.md` mtime 18:26:11, `table.md` mtime 18:36:48, `ls --time-style=full-iso`). Its negative-check method searched `preregistration.json` and `corpus/lessons/*.md` only (`.ufo/waves/round-47/w1/out/S8Proposals/table.md:37-41`), which is where *claims* are declared, not where *frontier* proposals are mined — those live in `.ufo/waves/round-*/w1/build_spec.py`. Fix: add `.ufo/waves/round-*/w1/build_spec.py` to the tracked set, and have the tracker read its own wave's seat items for proposal names.

**F3 — proposal 4's literal ask cannot be met by a round.** `considerthese/2026-10-26.md:35` asks for "one line per pinned engine … to each daily file"; `considerthese/` is read-only to rounds (`.ufo/waves/round-48/w1/build_spec.py:43` states the never-edit rule), so rounds 47 and 48 deliver the same substance as a wave-seat artifact instead (`.ufo/waves/round-47/w1/out/U2Pins/pins.md:16,25`; `.ufo/waves/round-48/w1/out/U2Pins.md:3`). A reader tracking the proposal by "daily file" will never see it. Fix: restate proposal 4 as "one line in the round report / daily-pass input", or have the daily pass consume the wave seat's `pins.md`.
