### Re-running live evidence (v5, round 48)

Supersedes `### Re-running live evidence` above for v5 work. The v4 bullets still describe a v4 receipt, and a committed v4 receipt still replays unchanged.

- Record with record-time verification: download the release archive and the model from revision-pinned https URLs into the work dir, then `python3 tools/kama-llamacpp-live.py --server <extracted llama-server> --model <gguf> --model-sha256 <hex> --server-asset-url <asset-url> --server-asset-sha256 <hex> --server-member <member-in-archive> --model-url https://huggingface.co/<org>/<repo>/resolve/<40-hex-commit>/<path> --server-archive <local-archive> --out <receipt>`. The model URL names a revision, never `main`, and `--model-sha256` is that revision's file digest.
- The archive is hashed against `--server-asset-sha256` and its member against the server binary's sha256 before the server starts; a mismatch exits 2 with a setup-error receipt and no server. A v5 receipt carries `provenance.verified` {`at`: `record`, …} beside `provenance.model.commit`.
- Replay: `python3 tools/kama-live-replay.py --receipt .ufo/waves/round-48/c2/live/receipt-1.json --work <empty-dir> --out <json>`. It re-acquires both inputs, verifies both digests before extracting anything, re-checks the member against `server.sha256` before executing it, passes the downloaded archive as `--server-archive` for a v5 receipt, and requires the rerun's stable projection to equal the receipt's, not only its check pass/fail. Exit 0 match, 1 ran and differs, 2 refused (`refused` names the reason).
- Compare two receipts offline, no network: `python3 tools/kama-live-replay.py --compare <a.json> <b.json>`. Ports, pids, timestamps, log text and the models body's `created` are ignored; a changed props, slots, health, models, metrics, loading, teardown or ownership surface is a mismatch. Exit 0 equal, 1 different, 2 unreadable.
- Offline check of the tools themselves: `python3 tools/kama-live-replay.py --selftest --scratch <existing-dir>` prints `KLR_SELFTEST pass=21 fail=0`, and `python3 tools/kama-llamacpp-live.py --selftest` prints `selftest: ok`.
- The graded form is `python3 tools/round48-live-replay-v5-audit.py --repo <clone> --tip <full-commit> --scratch <dir> [--out <receipt>]`: it replays from `git archive <tip>` under a scratch HOME and also measures the record-time refusals, the compare separation, two digest tampers and two URL swaps.
- It needs https to github.com and huggingface.co (about 74 MB) and a host that can exec the release `llama-server`, on a loopback port the tool picks. The round-48 receipts are b11909: `llama-b11909-bin-ubuntu-x64.tar.gz` sha256 `bf94332a…` with member `llama-b11909/llama-server`, and `stories260K.gguf` at `499bc882…` sha256 `270cba1b…`.
