## Limits of this draft

- I did not run the frozen counters. C1 needs ~75 MB of downloads and starts servers; its
  receipts are cited as the L1's (`measured/c1-build.out`), not as my measurement.
- `report-wave-order.py` decides on **author** time (a salvage or rebase rewrites committer
  time; here the salvage's two times differ by seconds). It counts only seat reports that
  are **added**; a fix commit that only restores a report is excluded deliberately.
- `seat-report-immutability.py` treats every `.ufo/waves/*/*/out/**/*.md` as a seat report
  (1806 paths at the build, ~2 min per run) and has no notion of an authorised edit besides
  the `.late-rewrite.md` sibling convention, which round 52 introduced.
- `claim-vs-receipt.py` is targeted: only pointer sentences inside `### Round-N measured
  results` sections. It is not a general claim checker.
- `cited-path-exists.py` finds 50 misses already at the base (fleet-relative report paths,
  brace-expansion pseudo-globs, `round-999`); only the `--base` delta is usable, which is
  why the build shows `new=1` and not 51.
