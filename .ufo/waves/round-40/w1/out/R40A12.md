## 3. Verdicts

| Mutant | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `M-sha` | **KILLED** (x2) | On `Dsha_spec` and `Dsha_run` (tampered-but-shape-correct session bytes), pristine says `met=False` exit 1; the sha-less mutant says `met=True` exit 0. The sha legs are load-bearing: they are the only gate that rejects a forged post-fix state. |

| `M-exists` | **SURVIVED** | `Dexists_run`/`Dexists_spec` are already rejected by the sha leg (`blob` None → sha_ok=0) and counts leg. Existence legs are diagnostic-only; subsumed. Coverage finding. |

**Baseline sanity:** pristine counter on `B` emits exactly the frozen `controls/c2-base.out` line (`spec_exists=0 run_exists=0 spec_sha_ok=0 run_sha_ok=0 counts_ok=0 met=False`) — baseline reproduced byte-for-byte from scratch, so no run is inconclusive.

**C2 claim direction:** at base, no mutant can spuriously flip `met` True (all mutants stay False on `B`) — the kill rule "met flips False → True" cannot be triggered by the pre-fix state even under weakened legs. On the genuine post-fix fixture `P`, pristine **and** all three mutants report `met=True` exit 0 — no false negative on the honest fix. The only falsification-relevant hole is the sha (: a counter lacking them would certify tampered evidence as met.
