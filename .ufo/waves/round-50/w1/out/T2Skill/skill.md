### Re-running live evidence (isolated replay, round 50)

- Record-time bound: `tools/kama-llamacpp-live.py` now writes `model_mapping` into every v5 receipt it produces (see the row above). Read it before trusting a receipt: `"mapped": true` plus a `sha256` equal to the model you think was served means the running server itself mapped that file; `"mapped": false` with a reason means the receipt makes no such claim.
- Replay: the ordinary invocation is unchanged — `python3 tools/kama-live-replay.py --receipt .ufo/waves/round-48/c2/live/receipt-1.json --work <empty-dir> --out <json>` — but the live child it starts is now isolated (`python3 -I`: no PYTHONPATH, no user site, no script directory) with only PATH, HOME, LANG, LC_ALL and a TMPDIR under `--work`, and both the replay and its child carry a parent-death signal. A rerun therefore reproduces regardless of what the caller's environment carried, and a caller that exports PYTHONPATH or LD_PRELOAD can no longer reach the graded children.
- The counter's own invocation, if you want the same shape by hand: start it from a tiny parent (`python3 -I -c 'import subprocess,sys; sys.exit(subprocess.call(sys.argv[1:]))' python3 -I tools/kama-live-replay.py ...`), give that parent an environment of exactly PATH, LANG, HOME, XDG_CACHE_HOME and TMPDIR, and plant the two canaries — `KAMA_R50_CANARY=<random>` and a `PYTHONPATH` directory holding a `sitecustomize.py` that appends `<pid> <argv>` to a marker file. A canary in `/proc/<server-pid>/environ`, or any line in the marker file, means the replay handed its environment on and the isolation is broken.
- Bind the server to the replay, outside the replay: while `<witness-dir>/ready.json` names `{port, pid}`, compare `sha256sum /proc/<pid>/exe` with the frozen b11909 `llama-server` digest, match the LISTEN inode in `/proc/net/tcp` and `/proc/net/tcp6` against `/proc/<pid>/fd`, walk the ppid chain up to the replay's pid, and read `/proc/<pid>/maps` for the model: the mapping's `dev` and `inode` must equal `stat` of its pathname and that pathname must hash to the model digest. Then `touch <witness-dir>/release` and expect the rerun receipt's `witness.released` true, its `child_pid` equal to the pid you observed and its `model_mapping` equal to what you saw.
- Tear the tree down the way the counter does: snapshot every descendant of the invoker (pid and the start time in field 22 of `/proc/<pid>/stat`), `kill -9` the invoker, and require every snapshotted process to be absent (or a reused pid with a different start time) and the port to refuse within 30 s — nothing should survive, replay, live tool or server.
- Graded form: `python3 tools/round50-replay-bind-audit.py --repo <clone> --tip <full-commit> --scratch <dir>` — needs https to github.com and huggingface.co (about 75 MB per replay), Linux `/proc` and a pids-capable host; 17.5 s on this host at `97c864be`. Exit 0 with `bind=1 teardown=1 newrow=1 bound=3/3 met=True` is the pass line.
