## 5. Which instrument would end it

The existing instruments are all narrow or token-bound: `tools/round35..40-statement/coverage/calibration-audit.py` each guard one prior defect; `tools/citation-provenance.py` (round 18) only verifies explicit `cite(rev, path, sha256=…)` tokens registered in `registry/required-citations.json`, not free prose. None scans lessons/reports/SCORECARD/SOURCES prose as-written.

**The instrument that would end the class is a generalized, pre-landing citation re-derivation linter over all authored text** — exactly operator proposal 1 (`considerthese/2026-10-25.md:27`) elevated from a daily read-only pass to a landing gate:

1. **Digests** — hash every committed evidence file a row/lesson/report cites (full-length sha256) at the cited revision; reject truncated/prefix-only/typo'd hex (kills the S472, `3ad0df68…`, and 39-hex classes).
2. **S-ids** — every `S\d{3,}` named in prose must resolve to a registry row present at that revision (kills id-drift / D2 classes).
3. **Paths** — every cited path must exist at the cited revision (kills dead-path and N3 classes).
4. **Counts** — word/line counts re-derived from committed blobs and compared (kills the 18-vs-20 and line-count classes).
5. **Run lines** — execute stated commands, compare stdout/exit (already done by `round41-scorecard-reconcile.py`).
6. **Schema binding** — field indices bound to headers (KQ48) and the linter itself carries frozen positive/negative fixture controls (kills the counter-schema and frozen-prose mismatch classes).

`tools/round41-scorecard-reconcile.py` is the working prototype of (1)–(5) for the SCORECARD head table; extending its extraction engine to lesson prose, fleet reports and SOURCES rows, and hooking it into the lane's landing gate (pre-push, next to the existing pre-report `ls-remote` gate) would break the relay: slips would be caught before landing instead of by the next closure. Round-41 C1's own controls prove the pattern is enforceable (junk variants each fail exactly the leg they fake).

## 6. Risks / limits

- The C1 counter verifies cited facts are true at the tip, **not** that they are sufficient evidence for the score (preregistration `out_of_counter_limit`); head-table reconciliation does not re-justify scores.
- `pin-watch` (C2) watches only row-backed pins; four version mentions in the corpus (Ollama v0.40.6, TGI v3.3.7, KoboldCpp 1.123.1, LocalAI v4.11.0) remain unwatched by design (`engine-pin-watch.md:49-57`) — the sweep is documented, not a gap.
- The generalized linter recommended in §5 does not exist yet; this round's counter is scoped to the SCORECARD head, so lessons/reports remain vulnerable to the same class until extended.
- The parked worktree remains on the mutation-sandbox lineage (r40 D5); all measurements above were taken from the fresh clone and are unaffected.
