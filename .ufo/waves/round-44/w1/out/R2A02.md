| 156 | (35, quality, outside) | mixed | **improved** | round-35-verifier-spotcheck.md:113 — same sentence pattern as the self side |

Proof that `improved` is representable: probe run above, all four keys `VALID` on those exact lines. Consequences if the draft ships as-is:

- (35, quality) is a **true agreement** (both sides improved) but lands in `other`, a disagreement bucket; the SCORECARD would name a disagreement that does not exist.
- (17, discernment) and (29, expansion) are true **underclaims** (self unchanged < outside improved) but land in `other`, hiding two real calibration misses from the overclaim/underclaim tally.
- The summary line the counter requires (`agreement=a/n overclaim=o underclaim=u other=x absent=k`) would be computed from the wrong world: `agree=61 … underclaim=8 other=15` instead of the honest `agreement=62/75 overclaim=3 underclaim=10 other=12 absent=15`.

Proposed fix: replace the four draft rows with the corresponding rows of `out/R2A02/fragment-corrected.tsv` (verdict `improved`, same repo/rev/path/line), then re-derive the SCORECARD `### Round-44 calibration ledger` table and summary line from the corrected ledger. The corrected full-key fragment passes 36/36 `VALID`; the counter-implied scorecard line becomes `agreement=62/75 overclaim=3 underclaim=10 other=12 absent=15`. The preregistration's C2 limits clause ("11 files carry more than one candidate line … attack seats re-read those") anticipated re-reading these lines; re-reading shows the single-verdict reading is the honest and the counter-accepted one, so `mixed` is not forced anywhere in my rounds.
