## Findings (frozen artifacts — reported, not touched)

- **F1 (counter fragility, medium).** `read_line` (`tools/round44-verdict-ledger-audit.py:91-107`) attributes a verdict word to the nearest *preceding* trend word on the same line, so any later verdict word on a multi-clause bullet folds into the trend: a quoted other-side verdict (outside-29 line 69) or an item-status word (self-27 line 121) makes the line mechanically `mixed`. The draft generator reproduced exactly this in both rows. Validation accepts the document's single verdict (`v in got`), so the predicate is still satisfiable — but `mixed` is not a reliable signal of "the document gives two verdicts". Proposed fix (for a future round's counter, never in place): attribute a verdict only to the *first* trend word on the line, or exclude segments inside backticks from `TOK`.
- **F2 (integration, informational).** With the draft baseline the counter reports `other=15`; a draft-only integration would place two `other` rows in the SCORECARD table for keys whose documents give one verdict each. Integrators should take F1's two resolutions, but the table/summary must be regenerated from whatever merged ledger is committed — `table_missing=26` currently equals the mismatch set, `summary=0` and `axes=0`.
- No defect found in the outside files for rounds 26–29 (all four exist at the lane base), so no `absent` row is warranted in my scope; `absent=15` in the summary comes from rounds 20–23/38 pairs outside my scope.
