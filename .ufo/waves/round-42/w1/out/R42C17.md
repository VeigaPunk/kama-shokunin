## 4. Do the quotes ground `teaches`? S478 no, S479 only by an unlabeled fragment

**S478 — the labeled quote grounds clause (a)'s premise, not the row's claim.** Line 1627 is inside the block-pool construction (`available_memory // bytes_per_block`, with `may_override_num_blocks` applied on 1628). It supports "from the block pool" and, if 1628 is read too, clause (c). It contains nothing about `max_model_len`, nothing about `int(max_concurrency * max_model_len)`, and nothing about a log line. The Gate itself states the two claim-bearing anchors were read but **not quoted** (`round-42.md:20`): "Also read, not quoted: line 2308 … and the log literal at line 2328." Clause (a) is the row's headline, and under rule 1 the labeled quote is supposed to ground it. It does not.

Worse for provenance: the 1627 anchor is the **staged** anchor, not a re-derived one. The staged amendment quotes that line under the prose "vLLM computes the served capacity from the memory it actually has" (`merge/kama/knowledge/corpus/knowledge/context-capacity-amendment-2026-10-23-r5.md:26` then code block at `:29`), and the lane's entry explicitly corrects that simplification — "the vLLM token figure is derived from `max_model_len`, not read straight off the block count" (`corpus/knowledge/context-capacity-engine-sides.md:37`; the corrected claim is stated at `:12` with `kv_cache_utils.py:2308`). The Gate therefore carries forward the one anchor the entry says it overturned, while declining to quote the anchor it adopted.

**S479 — grounds clause (b) only; the key-name clause is carried by an unlabeled fragment.** `if (meta.n_ctx_train > 0) {` grounds "only when positive". It does not contain `context_length`, so the row's headline ("under its own key `context_length`") is grounded by line 2379, which the Gate gives verbatim with its line number but in backticks and after the words "followed on line 2379 by" — i.e. it is present in the Gate but is not the Gate's quote, and (see §8) the counter cannot see it at all because backticked fragments are not inspected. A spot-check that re-locates only the labeled quote verifies the guard, not the claim.

This is the `false provenance` shape the critic seat exists for: every field is filled and every field is true, but the field that rule 1 exists to make checkable — *this quote grounds this claim* — is satisfied only for the premise.

## 5. Would a quote from 2308 or 2328 be a stronger anchor for S478?

- **Line 2308 — yes, strongest single anchor.** `return int(max_concurrency * max_model_len), max_concurrency` is 5 words, unique in the file, inside `get_kv_cache_capacity` (def 2300, with `max_model_len` bound at 2304), and it is the exact expression the row's `teaches` reproduces. So "next to the declared length" (clause (b)) is not visible from 2328 alone; a 2328-2332 span (~19 words) or the 2329 fragment (8 words) plus the argument list is what grounds clause (b).
- **Recommendation:** make 2308 the located quote (grounds the claim), add 2329 as a second located quote (grounds clause (b) together with 2331-2333), and keep 1627 as a *named premise* rather than the quote. That keeps the whole bullet ≤25 words per fragment and makes each clause re-locatable in one grep.
