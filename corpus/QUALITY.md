# kama corpus — quality bar

Criteria apply to new admissions and explicit corrections. Existing dated
knowledge is history, not proof of a present responder. Criteria are added as
new rows; supersession names the old criterion instead of erasing it.

| ID | Admit only when | Reject or limit when | Motivating lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| KQ1 | A current endpoint claim includes the actual authorized probe command, sampled time/revision, complete verdict and real exit code. | A configured URL, listener, `/health` answer, completion success, or September MODEL_OK is offered as present served-model identity. | `lessons/round-5.md` Gate admits 1–3; `lessons/round-1.md` identity evidence. |
| KQ2 | Pin provenance (`file_match`, resolver kind), endpoint verdict and effective runtime selection are separate claims. | An inherited/raw fallback is called a file-derived effective URL, or a correct pin is treated as a live positive control. Never inspect environment values to fill that gap in this run. | `lessons/round-5.md` admits 1–2 and rejected positive-control/environment claims. |
| KQ3 | Historical examples have explicit dates/supersession, and active instructions use existing registered surfaces. Corrections preserve old corpus bytes. | A deleted unit, janitor/provider file, old pid/count, or missing source-line anchor is presented as current executable authority. Re-anchor live sources; mark retired observations historical. | `lessons/round-5.md` admits 2–4; `lessons/round-4-recensus.md` named stale-prose debt. |
| KQ4 | Freshness scope is explicit: snapshot evidence compares authorized invocations, and the exact command/exit distinguishes drift from probe failure. | Invocation history is called periodic monitoring, fixtures are called real-runtime integration, or endpoint restoration is claimed from a documentation measurement. | `lessons/round-2.md`; `lessons/round-5.md` measurement/smoke limits. |
| KQ5 | External service/config actions remain an operator handoff; route/secret/eval findings go to the province owner. Reports contain nonsecret shape evidence only. | A training admission requires enabling services, repointing consumers, installing timers, copying config/environment/session/journal bodies, or fabricating receipts/verification. | `lessons/round-1-safety.md`; `lessons/round-5.md` rejected candidates and continuation debt. |

## Review boundary

Author and parent-dispatched advisor agreement are not an outside spot-check.
`lessons/round-5.md` records the missing non-author admit/reject review; no
criterion or local gate pass certifies promotion, native judgment, or a
completed training round.
