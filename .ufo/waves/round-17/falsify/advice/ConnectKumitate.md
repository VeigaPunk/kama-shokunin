**Ask kumitate for a `provenance` sidecar minted by the producer at run time; demote the lane recorder to a verifier of that sidecar.** `ufo-swarm-wave.mjs run` already persists `swarm-registry.json` + `swarm-dispatch.json` sidecar-first in one critical section (`kumitate SKILL.md:41-44`); add to the emitted payload's existing `policy` block the policy-file `path`/`sha256`/note `version`, plus `taskCall_sha256` under one named canonicalization, stamped `recorded_at`. kama's `dispatch-provenance.py` keeps env-binding, attempt counting and lane-local cross-checks — its `bound_task_call_sha256` (`falsify/provenance-ledger.json` entries[1]) becomes checkable against the producer's own digest with zero new tooling.
