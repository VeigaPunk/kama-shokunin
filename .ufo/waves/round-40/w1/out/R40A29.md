## Finding

Two-space-indented rows are recognized and validated, not ignored. This is a coverage finding for structural census alone, not a contract change recommendation: the frozen C1 calibration audit must remain responsible for the semantic predicate. The temporary validator/test files are outside lane evidence and no mutation required restoration of lane bytes.
