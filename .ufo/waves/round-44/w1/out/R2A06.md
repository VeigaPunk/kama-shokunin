So on a line carrying e.g. `improved` plus a quoted `unchanged`, **both** `mixed` and `improved` are counter-valid — the counter cannot adjudicate which is the document's verdict. That adjudication is this attack's job (preregistration C2 `limits`: "the counter checks that a cited line carries the verdict ... not that it is the file's verdict sentence rather than a quotation").

## Counter observations (finding, not fix — counters are frozen)

- `ok = v in got` membership semantics (round44-verdict-ledger-audit.py:182) means a line carrying a trend's two distinct words validates *both* a `mixed` row and a single-word row for either word; conversely the counter cannot catch a row that picks the wrong word, nor a `mixed` row whose document gave a single verdict. This is within the preregistration's declared limits and is why attack seats re-read; F1 is the concrete instance in my slice.
- Negation/quotation blindness (`not improved`, `The author's 'unchanged'`) is declared in the preregistration limits; F1 is the concrete instance in my slice.
