## Limits of this draft

- I ran **no** claim counter. The C1/C2 receipts are the L1's, cited as committed bytes
  (copies in this directory); the two checks I wrote are the HASH half of the reconcile
  instrument and one new anchor check.
- Row 2 compares two different populations on purpose: the prototype's 51 (`.ufo/` tokens in
  two surfaces, lane tree only, restored section cut) and the round-53 counter's 16 (every
  lane root plus the fleet's `scripts/`/`templates/` in three surfaces, lane then fleet tree).
  I did not re-list the prototype's 50 tokens one by one; what I established is its parser's
  scope (`grep -c fleet` → 0) and its bucket rule.
- Row 6's fix is textual. It needs a content-correspondence row (KQ14 shape), not a counter,
  and I say so rather than dressing a `grep` as an instrument.
- Row 7 is a note-level defect **inside** the frozen contract's tolerance: the counter's
  `untracked` rule is satisfied, `bad=0 stale=0` stand, and `met=True` is not challenged. What
  is false is the reason the row gives a reader.
- C2's own limit 5 (`corpus/lessons/round-53.md` §Limits) still holds: the parser checks that
  a cited path exists, not that the citing sentence is right. Rows 6 and 7 are instances of
  exactly that gap.
- The receipts run the counters with `--repo .` (the lane working tree) while the
  preregistered command names `<lane clone>`. The counters read blobs by `git show <tip>:…`,
  so the named tip's bytes are what was measured; recorded here, not raised as a row.
