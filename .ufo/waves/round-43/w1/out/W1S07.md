## Findings (frozen artefacts — reported, not fixed)

**F1 — the three counters were invisible to their own instrument gate.**
`SKILL.md` at `895793e0` is 1375 lines; its last registry section is
`SKILL.md:1364` `### Round-34 instrument registry rows`, and `grep -niE "round.?4[0-3]"
SKILL.md` returns nothing. The three round-42 counters are documented only in prose in
`SCORECARD.md:2252-2254` (claims) and `SCORECARD.md:2277-2279` (measured results), which
`gate-registry-check.py` does not accept as registration (`:16-21`). Shown by
`gate-baseline-at-freeze.out` (19 `unregistered` lines, three of them these counters).
Proposed fix: insert `rows.md` after `SKILL.md:1374` under
`| Name | Kind / entry command | Exit map | Self-test / pinned values | Write scope |`
plus a separator row; the remaining 16 counters need rows from their own seats before C1's
`unreg` can reach 0.

**F2 — naming a sibling counter inside a row registers it, and can add a `flags` mismatch.**
The population pattern is `tools/round[0-9]+-[A-Za-z0-9._-]+\.py`
(`tools/gate-registry-check.py:69-71`), so a row that mentions
`tools/round41-scorecard-reconcile.py` in its write-scope cell registers that counter; the
round-43 `flags` leg then requires every `--flag` in that row's Kind cell to be declared by
`round41-scorecard-reconcile.py`, which declares only `--repo --tip --out`
(`tools/round41-scorecard-reconcile.py:221-223`) and not `--seed`.
Measured with full paths: `registered 17 unregistered 14 defective 0`; with bare filenames
(what `rows.md` ships): `registered 15 unregistered 16 defective 0`. Proposed fix: keep
sibling counters out of a row's cells by bare filename until their own rows exist; when the
round-41 rows land, name only `--repo --tip --out` for the scorecard counter.

**F3 — the capacity counter's verdict depends on a file outside the evaluated repository.**
`tools/round42-capacity-audit.py:76` sets
`CENSUS = "/home/vgpnk/Projects/shokunin/scripts/sources-census.sh"` and `:222-228` runs it
on a temporary export; a missing or moved host copy silently turns the `census` leg to 0
(exit 1, "not met") rather than "could not evaluate". The file exists on this host
(35466 bytes, mtime 2026-10-19). Proposed fix (frozen counter, so finding only): treat an
absent census script as an evaluate error, not a failed leg.

**F4 — two non-`2` failure paths exist in the capacity counter.** A `timeout=300`
(`:226`) or a missing `tar` propagates uncaught past
`except (RuntimeError, subprocess.CalledProcessError)` at `:244`, so a "could not evaluate"
condition is reported as exit 1 with a traceback and no `--out` file. The row states this
explicitly. Proposed fix: extend the tuple at `:244` with `subprocess.TimeoutExpired` and
`OSError`.

**F5 — receipt `exit=` lines are harness wrappers.** No counter prints `exit=`;
`base.c1.out:43`, `base.c2.out:7` and `base.c3.out:16` carry a trailing `exit=N` appended
by the round-42 runner. A reader who treats those lines as counter output inherits the right
codes here, but the rows cite source lines instead so they cannot drift from the bytes.
