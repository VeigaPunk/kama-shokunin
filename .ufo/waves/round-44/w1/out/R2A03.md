### Counter blind spot (frozen artifact — a finding, not fixed in place)

`tools/round44-verdict-ledger-audit.py` `read_line` attributes every verdict token on a line to the nearest preceding trend word with no quotation/negation awareness, and the single-verdict check is membership, so a line carrying a quoted second token validates both `mixed` and the document's true single verdict. An automatic last-hit generator that prefers `mixed` when ≥2 tokens are present will systematically misstate documents that quote other verdicts inline (three for three in this slice). Suggested for the counter's owner: derive the row verdict from the bullet-head token (the verdict word immediately following the trend name) or require the generator to record all candidate tokens in the note; the same-line rule itself cannot be fixed by citation choice here — no line in any of the three documents gives the trend exactly one verdict word (tokenizer receipt).
