## Round24 continuation — class witnesses, error boundaries and pairing scope

All standing criteria, including KQ20 and KQ25, remain binding. KQ35 qualifies KQ34; this continuation adds no criterion and supersedes none.

- **Paired obligations require substantive, class-specific witnesses.** A declared blanket-refusal operator must turn a correct normal verdict (exit 0 or 1) on an audit-sufficient control into refusal (exit 2), with its named reason. A declared unavailable-evidence acceptance operator must turn the correct content-derived refusal (exit 2) into a valid verdict (exit 0 or 1) on a genuinely unavailable required-object control. One class discharges no obligation of the other.
- **Reason loss is sensitivity, not acceptance.** KQ34's pinned reason remains observable: a valid, attributed same-exit refusal whose named reason changes may expose reason-loss sensitivity. It does not show that an unavailable object was accepted. An unchanged normal exit also does not, by itself, establish that the verdict document is unchanged or that a substantive exit changed; preserve its detail separately.
- **Runtime faults and invalid experiments earn no kill or obligation credit.** A traceback, internal error, timeout, harness leak, malformed verdict, invalid exit or out-of-envelope failure is could-not-evaluate, not a semantic mutant kill. Such a fault must not be re-labelled as ordinary same-exit reason-loss sensitivity. Preserve the actual class rows and unexpected rows without widening the attribution envelope.
- **Completed measurement is not admission.** Probe exit 0 records completion of a finite, named inventory on pinned inputs, not admission of this round. A valid finite survivor remains a completed measurement. Report each survivor with its reason; an undocumented survivor discharges no obligation.
- **No trade is waived by disclosure.** `AXES.md` §Working the axes (fleet root, lines 34–36) still requires every claim to verify and no axis to be harmed. Losing a correct runnable workflow is not an admissible trade merely because it is disclosed.
- **Original and prospective expectations remain distinct.** The original `preregistration.json` `expected_after[3]` is withdrawn/not-achieved, never retroactively passed. `oracle-amendment-1.json` is the prospective correction. The graded 47-cell manifest remains byte-identical (`d86b7bea9c49c3d2fcd026a3a1ffb502871d15a2c8db624eba9745e664f14cd1`).
