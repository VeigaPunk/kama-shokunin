## Finding (not fixed in place; counter and drafts are frozen)

- **Counter looseness, `tools/round44-verdict-ledger-audit.py:96-121` (`read_line`)**: any verdict token after the last trend word is attributed to that trend, including tokens inside a *quotation of another side's verdict*. `out-12.md:123` therefore yields `{improved, unchanged}` and a `mixed` verdict at that line is accepted by `main()`'s `len(got) >= 2` branch, even though the file gives exactly one outside verdict. Proposed fix (proposal only): strip backtick/quote-delimited spans and `supersede*` clauses before tokenizing, or require `mixed` to be carried by two verdict words in distinct clauses that are not marked as superseded/quoted. This is why the draft's automatic pass emitted `mixed` here; my row uses the semantically correct single verdict, which the counter also accepts.
- No defect found in the self reports or outside files for rounds 10–13: each round names all three trends in one trend-verdict section, one verdict word per trend per side.
