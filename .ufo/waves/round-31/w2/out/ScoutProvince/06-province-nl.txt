40| **Refuses:** model quality/eval (kengaku), credential material & auth flows (kagi),
41| dispatch/route mechanism (kumitate), remote web surfaces (kake). Boundary rule: kama
42| answers *"is the thing at this baseURL the model this config believes it is, right

45| **Write boundary (hard):** kama never writes to `~/.config`, `~/.omp`, or systemd
46| units. Those are operator-owned; kama *detects and reports* stale pins, and the
47| operator repoints them. Rationale: a lane that silently repoints a credential-bearing
48| consumer config would be acting outside its authority (see
49| `corpus/lessons/round-1-safety.md`).
