=> An ordinary root-owned host library is NOT audit-capable; ld.so refuses the
   audit load ("undefined symbol: la_version; ignored") and maps nothing extra.
   The battery's m-ldaudit-foreign vector-analysis claim is grounded: the
   LD_AUDIT route cannot plant a foreign non-audit DSO into the counter's child.
   Only a purpose-built DSO exporting la_version gets mapped (RUN 2), and the
   counter's envnames leg records env-var NAMES — an attacker-controlled
   LD_AUDIT value would still require a writable, audit-capable DSO path.
