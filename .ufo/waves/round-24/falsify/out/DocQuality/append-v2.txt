## Round24 continuation — paired-obligation witnesses, no-trade anchor, withdrawn
   original, and pairing-coverage bound (append-only)

This continuation adds no criterion and rewrites no row. KQ35 stands as written; the
following fixes scope KQ35's own wording left open, and each item is answerable from
committed bytes.

- **KQ35's paired obligations are discharged by a substantive class witness.** For the
  complete-state obligation the witness is a control cell that must carry a normal
  verdict — exit 0 or 1 — answering instead with a refusal, exit 2; that is the
  observable shape of a refusal that metadata alone produced. For the
  unavailable-state obligation the witness is a control cell whose required object is
  genuinely unavailable, so the correct answer is a content-derived refusal, exit 2,
  answered instead with a valid verdict, exit 0 or 1; that is the observable shape of an
  unavailable object being accepted. A witness is per obligation and per class: a
  complete-state witness discharges no unavailable-state obligation, and the reverse.
- **A lost or altered pinned refusal reason is observable sensitivity, never
  availability-acceptance credit.** KQ34 requires the refusal reason to be pinned, and a
  mutant that drops or rewrites that reason is detected and is reported. Such a detection
  shows that the reason-pinning check fires; it does not show that an unavailable object
  was accepted, so it earns no availability-acceptance credit and cannot stand as the
  unavailable-state witness above. Diagnostic-only outcomes — a traceback, an internal
  `could-not-evaluate`, a timeout, a harness leak, or a failure outside the mutant's
  declared envelope — earn 0 credit for any obligation and are reported separately from
  the class witnesses.
- **A probe's exit 0 is a completed measurement, not an admission.** It records that a
  finite, named inventory was measured over a pinned population; it is not itself an
  admission of the round's claims. An obligation is discharged only by a substantive
  class witness on a named control of that class. A surviving mutant is reported with its
  named reason and credits nothing; the count of survivors not so accounted for is 0.
- **The no-trade rule is unchanged and is anchored here.** `AXES.md` §Working the axes
  (fleet root): a round is admitted only if every claim verifies **and no axis is
  harmed** — a trade anywhere is not an improvement. Disclosure of a trade does not
  waive the rule. A correct runnable workflow lost and excused as a disclosed trade is
  not an admissible round outcome.
- **The initial expectation is withdrawn, not achieved.** The initial registration's
  blanket unknown-citation expectation (`.ufo/waves/round-24/preregistration.json`
  `expected_after[3]`) is withdrawn and not achieved; it is retained as the original
  view and reported as withdrawn, never as satisfied. The prospective correction is
  `.ufo/waves/round-24/oracle-amendment-1.json`, and the 47-cell manifest
  `tests/report-audit/promisor-grid-expectations.json` (`d86b7bea…`) is the frozen
  graded authority and is not rewritten.
- **Pairing coverage is bounded, and the bound is stated rather than implied.** In the
  frozen 47-cell manifest, 42 of 47 cells participate in a plain-twin pair; five `policy`
  cells carry no twin inside the frozen 47 — PG21, PG43, PG46 and PG47 (exit 2) and
  PG48 (exit 0). Those cells are graded by their own per-cell exit, kinds, defects and
  reason class, not by a twin verdict equality. Private plain metadata-only companions
  for those cells are planned outside the frozen 47; if and when they run they are
  expected to report four paired refusals (PG21, PG43, PG46, PG47) at 2 of 2 rows each
  with no verdict-document equality available, because a refusal emits no verdict
  document, and PG48 clean at 0 of 0. That expectation is not yet a measurement. The
  companions add no primary score, change no manifest cell and no expectation byte, and
  do not convert the frozen 47 into a paired population.
