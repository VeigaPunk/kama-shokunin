## The original prose clause and the encoded original cell view are not the same thing

- **Original prose clause** — the literal rule in
  `preregistration.json` `expected_after[3]`: an unknown citation in a
  promisor-backed repository is unknown. It is a *rule*, and it is superseded
  for the graded cells by the filed amendment; the original claim stays
  **withdrawn / not-achieved** either way, with its original cells and history
  retained.
- **Encoded original cell view** — the manifest's per-cell `met_original`
  field, which differs from the amended view on exactly seven cells.

Neither view is upgraded by this round. No summary may present a live failure
as a withdrawal: a cell is *withdrawn* only where the filed amendment
supersedes its original expectation. By that test, of the items above,
**PG22, PG23, PG24, PG30, PG41, PG40 and F06** are withdrawn under the original
clause, while **PG28** is not (its amended expectation comes from the
amendment's prospective expectation for the unhydrated blobless lane) and
**PG34** is not (a non-promisor no-harm twin whose amended expectation is
different from its original one — an expectation change, not a withdrawal).
Ordinary live failures stay ordinary live failures.
