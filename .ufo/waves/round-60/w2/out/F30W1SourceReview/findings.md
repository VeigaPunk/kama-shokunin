Scope: W1 S519–S523 drafts and opened-source gates, against frozen round-60 C1/C2 boundaries. Lane source untouched. Evidence read from W1 seat outputs; I did not independently re-fetch URLs or run the census.

## Finding

**Conditional admission, not wholesale approval.** S519, S520 and S521 are admissible in their stated narrow scopes, subject to integration recheck of exact 11-column rows and duplicate/supersession gates. S522 is a likely scope-duplicate refusal/fold candidate; S523 is inadmissible as an A-tier independent source and should be treated as run-specific own-work evidence (or refused from SOURCES if its contract cannot justify A). These are recommendations, not edits or a change to frozen claim verdicts.

- **S519 — admit, narrowly.** Gate records release page, checksum asset, GitHub asset metadata and downloaded bytes, all agreeing on archive SHA256/size. Its scope is archive identity only, explicitly excluding behavior. Draft row names 2 URLs in one source cell but preserves clear anchor to checksum line. Keep tier A scoped to publisher's digest statement; do not let binary hash or runtime claim inherit that tier. `used-by` in K01 draft says the release pin is used, while S519 row's used-by field in the gate row is `—`; reconcile at integration (the K01 source-rows text itself says used-by names the pin). Duplicate scan was evidenced against base; repeat against integrated registry.
- **S520 — admit only the documented variable/default scope.** W1 gate opened floating FAQ and version-tag source and explicitly documents `.md`/`.mdx` discrepancy. Scope safely describes bind/model-store/cloud/keep-alive/context; however gate's actual quote evidence is detailed only for bind and model-store, not cloud-disable, keep-alive or context. Before admitting those latter teaches, require their precise locatable quotations from the opened snapshots. Draft row says 90d while gate argues `on-release` for an undated page and version-bounded source; reconcile recheck policy. Keep tier A scoped to the cited first-party statements, never systemd precedence or observed runtime.
- **S521 — admit, narrow.** W1 opened tag-pinned `docs/import.mdx` (the `.md` URL is 404) and located the `ollama create` recipe. Scope is distinct from S013's quantization/shard scope; same-URL behavior gets W04 across scopes and E04 within scope per the reported scratch checks. Row is not evidence for default tag/name behavior; its own report correctly says no `:latest` claim is sourced. Ensure the row URL is the actual tag-pinned `.mdx` source if its anchor depends on that version.
- **S522 — refuse or fold into S010; do not admit as drafted.** Gate explicitly finds the same canonical URL as S010 and says S522's scope (two Notes-field meanings on `/v1/models` and `/{model}`) is a subset of S010's broader “Ollama OpenAI-compatible surface”. Different anchor/version and additional route detail may warrant an S010 update, but do not by themselves establish distinct scope under the frozen registry's `tier`-scoped contract. A separate narrowed row would require an integration policy basis and explicit duplicate-gate disposition, not simply a version pin. The live capture's ID/envelope observations are separate own-work evidence, not source support for S522's doc scope.
- **S523 — refuse A; consider own-work row only under justified tier.** A single committed receipt and a counter pass establish one run's recorded observations, not an independent authoritative source. The W1 source cell includes tool files, pin and receipt, while the `used-by` points to counter and knowledge entry; this is self-referential and does not establish an independent basis for tier A. It can support explicitly run-bounded observations under the lane's own-work convention if the registry permits it, but tier must reflect that convention and remain bounded to the receipt, with no general Ollama behavior inference. Frozen C1 already limits meaning to one receipt/version/model; this critique does not alter C1/C2 predicates.
