## 6. Defects found (frozen/staged material — reported, not fixed)

**D1 — `E09`: staged recheck cells carry prose; the two rows the round wants would fail the gate.**
`/home/vgpnk/Projects/shokuknow/merge/kama/SOURCES.append.md:107` (S430) and `:108` (S431) have
`recheck = 90d — re-open the release page and expect …`; same shape at `:115–:120` (S438–S443).
Command: `validate-mirror.sh assimilation/inputs/staged-rows-renumbered.md …` → `E09 S480`,
`E09 S481`, `census_exit=1` (`assimilation/proof-staged-rows.out`).
Scale: 132 of 142 staged rows have a `NNd — …` recheck cell; the lane registry has 0 of 220
(`assimilation/staged-row-status.tsv`; lane check: `python3` over `corpus/SOURCES.md`).
Fix: set the `recheck` cell to `90d` alone (or `on-release`) and move the re-open expectation into
the `anchor` cell or the lesson's §Gate — the census accepts only an ISO date, `NNd`, or a symbolic
cadence (`scripts/sources-census.sh:28`; contract header `:24–:35`).

**D2 — `E01`: 90 of 142 staged rows are not 11-cell rows** (interior `|` in anchor/teaches/recheck).
Command: `validate-mirror.sh assimilation/inputs/neg-rows-malformed-batch.md …` →
`E01 S480 fields=12 … E01 S484 fields=12` plus shifted `E11` lines
(`assimilation/proof-neg-malformed-batch.out`), i.e. the extra pipes shift `used-by` into another
column. Fix: strip or escape `|` inside cells before append; W1C08's S480/S481 rows are 11 cells
and W1C08 records “No `|` occurs inside any cell.”

**D3 — `E03`: 9 staged ids already belong to the same document in the lane registry**
(`S330 S333 S334 S335 S346 S347 S352 S353 S363`; `collision-detail.tsv`). Fix: drop those rows —
their URL maps to the existing lane id (e.g. S330→S330, S335→S335) — and never renumber them to
fresh ids, which would duplicate the document.

**D4 — `E11`: staged rows' `used-by` names an amendment that is not in the lane tree.**
`:107`/`:108` point at
`corpus/knowledge/runtime-health-surface-matrix-amendment-2026-10-24-r12.md` (`:115–:120` at the
`-r13` file); `ls corpus/knowledge | grep -i amendment` at the freeze shows only
`prefix-cache-reuse-models-amendment-2026-10-23.md`,
`served-identity-crosswalk-amendment-2026-10-24-r16.md`,
`sglang-docs-version-pinning-amendment-2026-10-23.md`. Fix: repoint `used-by` at the C3 entry
`corpus/knowledge/runtime-health-surface-processes.md` (what W1C08's rows do), or land the
amendment files; appending the rows without either gives `E11`.

**D5 — duplicate documents inside the staged slice.** S431 and S441 are both PR 58552; S438 also
appears as S423, S439 as S427, S440 as S420/S454/S462, and S440's URL is already lane row S004.
Fix: collapse by URL before renumbering (§3 map already does this).

**D6 — the quarantine slice is stale by id.** 6 of its 7 ids (S200, S202, S210, S211, S224, S230)
are absent from the lane registry; only S206 resolves. A quarantine step keyed on those ids would

(S200→S335, S211→S346, S230→S329, S206→S206) and record S202, S210, S224 as no-longer-registered
(a register-side correction, not a lane edit).
