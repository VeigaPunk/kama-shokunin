## Deltas from the uncommitted worktree draft (`corpus/QUALITY.md:177`)

The worktree draft is directionally right and already narrows KQ32 explicitly.
Six changes make it consistent with M1 at the measured revision:

1. **Element completeness is explicit.** The draft credits a mutant only if the
   grid kills one, but never requires the mutant population to cover every
   element M1 names. At `245a37f` there are 24 mutants and none covers the NUL
   record element the lesson itself names: `round_commits` uses `-z` + `%x00`
   (`tools/report-landing-audit.py:183`) and F25 grades subject-record
   injection, yet no mutant touches `-z`, `%x00` or the record split
   (`grep -c '"-z"' tests/report-audit/isolation-mutants.py` → 0). The row now
   makes a promise channel with no mutant a rejection; the base instance is
   named.
2. **Kill attribution is required.** The mutant runner credits a kill on any
   nonzero grid exit (`isolation-mutants.py:111`, `"killed": rc != 0`). That is
   the KQ20/KQ25 failure mode in isolation clothing. M21 is killed only by
   `C08-cr-only-report` (a control); a single-element `-z` removal is killed by
   15 violations including a blanket `unexpected git log output (7 fields)` on
   two controls and 13 rows. The row now demands pasted failed rows and limits
   channel credit to the channel's own row failing with its named fault.
3. **Defensive-only needs dominance, not survival.** The draft says "elements
   no mutant kills are labelled defensive-only". At `245a37f` that licenses a
   false label: `--no-lazy-fetch` (M02) survives the grid, yet a
   `remote.<n>.partialclonefilter`-only promisor remote passes every refusal
   and runs `core.sshCommand` once the flag is removed. The row requires a
   fault row aimed at the channel that passes without the element because a
   named dominating refusal runs first.
4. **Premise wording matches the code.** The draft requires the premise to be
   "asserted"; eight fault builders have no `require()` call (`F02`, `F16`,
   `F17`, `F18`, `F19`, `F24`, `F25`, `F26`), including all raw-commit
   fixtures. Their faults are constructed and fail closed through `g(check=True)`
   or direct byte writes, not asserted at runtime. The row accepts an
   established premise and rejects a claim of uniform `require()`.
5. **Counts are bound to the measured revision.** The draft quotes nothing, but
   the lesson it cites does: `corpus/lessons/round-23.md:64` says "8
   tracebacks" while the base grid reports 9. The row requires counts to name
   the measured revision and digests to be pinned (population
   `83a84702251c62c6990c8222ea6c4276e09db05344b3c2c0d862a857e50ea2f1`).
6. **Channel scope is not self-selecting.** "Only the channels in its committed
   fault grid" lets a gate choose what it is graded on. The row adds channels a
   probe shows violating the promise, with the open-row alternative; that is
   how the partialclonefilter channel enters without lifting KQ32.
