## Reconstruction and causal chain

The exact edit that introduced the divergence is bounded by the old unit backup (`qwen-coder.service.bak.1788033391`, old `--port 8080`, mtime 2026-08-29 16:56:36 -0300 / encoded epoch 2026-08-29 19:56 UTC) and the replacement live unit (mtime 2026-09-08 20:43:13 -0300, `--port 34113`). The replacement edit did more than move the listener: it changed ctx-size 32768→16384 and parallel 64→2, added the full-path alias and `--cors-origins http://127.0.0.1:8080`, and added/changed serving flags, as shown by the two unit files. OpenCode never followed: every inspected backup and the current `~/.config/opencode/opencode.json:158` retained `http://localhost:8080/v1`; the specific consumer-side line where divergence remained possible is that `baseURL` assignment. Docker/Jitsi subsequently claimed `127.0.0.1:8080` when `docker-jitsi-meet-jvb-1` was created at `2026-09-15T01:31:55.116832736Z`, corroborating why 8080 is now a foreign service, not proving why the model was moved. A live endpoint/consumer reconciliation probe (or equivalent endpoint-health check wired into the edit/deploy workflow) **would have caught this at edit time** by comparing the declared base URL with the actual model endpoint and model identity; this is the freshness lesson.
