## Round24 continuation — no-trade, kill-credit and pairing scope (append-only)

This continuation adds no criterion and rewrites no row. KQ35 stands as written; the
following only fixes scope that KQ35's own wording left open, and each item is
answerable from committed bytes.

- **Kill credit is verdict-shaped, never diagnostic-shaped.** KQ20 and KQ25 remain in
  force for the round-24 promisor contract and are not superseded here. A mutant earns
  kill credit only from a verdict difference on a control cell it is declared to
  discriminate: a crash, a traceback, an internal `could-not-evaluate`, a timeout or a
  harness leak is could-not-evaluate and credits no kill, and a failure on a cell
  outside the mutant's declared rows does not kill that mutant. Diagnostic failure and
  verdict failure are reported separately, and the actual class rows a mutant
  discriminates are preserved as reported.
- **The no-trade rule is unchanged and is anchored here.** `AXES.md` §Working the axes
  (fleet root): a round is admitted only if every claim verifies **and no axis is
  harmed** — a trade anywhere is not an improvement. Disclosure of a trade does not
  waive the rule. A correct runnable workflow lost and excused as a disclosed trade is
  not an admissible round outcome.
- **The initial expectation is withdrawn, not achieved.** The initial registration's
  blanket unknown-citation expectation (`.ufo/waves/round-24/preregistration.json`
  `expected_after[3]`) is withdrawn and not achieved; it is retained as the original
  view and reported as withdrawn, never as satisfied. The prospective correction is
  `.ufo/waves/round-24/oracle-amendment-1.json`, and the 47-cell manifest
  `tests/report-audit/promisor-grid-expectations.json` (`d86b7bea…`) is the frozen
  graded authority and is not rewritten.
- **Pairing coverage is bounded, and the bound is stated rather than implied.** The
  frozen manifest encodes a plain twin for 42 of its 47 cells; five `policy` cells carry
  no twin inside the frozen 47 — PG21, PG43, PG46 and PG47 (exit 2) and PG48 (exit 0).
  Those cells are graded by their own per-cell exit, kinds, defects and reason class,
