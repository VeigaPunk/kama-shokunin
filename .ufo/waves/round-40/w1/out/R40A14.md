## Notes / risks for the integrator (not defects in the evidence)

- `counts_ok` only checks the two counts; it does not itself compare spec item names to the registry. The correspondence holds for the authentic files, so no unverifiability arises — but the receipt proves counts, not membership identity. If the L1 wants the receipt to prove the 36 items are the registry members, that would require a counter extension (out of scope; not needed to verify C2 as frozen).
- The counter reads `round-38/w1/spec.json` and `run.json` at the new tip; both files must be **committed** (not merely present in a worktree) or `met` stays False. The restorable source bytes are the kama-r38 clone's files; regenerating from scratch risks byte drift and hash mismatch, so copying those exact bytes is the safe path.
- `run.json` also embeds a registry copy with the same 36 member keys (agentIds null there); consistent.
- No member of the registry is a non-spawn entry; every seat has a report; nothing in the registry would make the promised counts unreachable.
