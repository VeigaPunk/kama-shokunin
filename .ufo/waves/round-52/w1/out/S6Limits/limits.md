## Evidence scope

The R52 design closes the specific *running-image-at-a-path* substitution that defined the R51 C1 shortfall: the observed image is a zero-link, sealed memfd, not the copied/linked/renamed disk member. It does **not** prove the memfd was populated from the downloaded archive; the frozen preregistration expressly preserves that limit. It also does not close R50 PATH/model-mapping/early-kill limits, network/prerelease dependence, or Linux `/proc` dependency. These are design/contract classifications from the frozen sources, not independent runtime results.
