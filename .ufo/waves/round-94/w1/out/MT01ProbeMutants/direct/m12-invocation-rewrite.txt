=========================================================
The frozen counter's T01-rewrite cell uses THREE separate probe invocations (SEEN is per process), so
it cannot see a memo key that drops ctime. The distinguishing input is a rewrite inside ONE
invocation, between two candidates that name the same inode.

scripts/m12-race.py <probe> <slow-invalid> <big-valid> builds one `files` invocation with
