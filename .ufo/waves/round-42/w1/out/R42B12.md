## 4. Defect found inside the excluded population (22 tokens, one file)

`.ufo/waves/round-25/a2-build/advice/ExecRawReplicate.md` lines **21–31** (the "Observed table" rows K1–K8, X1–X3) carries **22 distinct 64-hex tokens, 26 occurrences**, every one classified `wrong_unflagged`: each shares its first **16–17** hex characters with the real digest of the corresponding committed raw file and diverges in the tail, so it hashes nothing.

Concrete case, `ExecRawReplicate.md:21` (K1 row):

```
cited  props.sha256 = 064368843fe8eb55766a7b22d175e153f0d0c199ae6e9ac1a238ee59c93adfa3
real   sha256(.ufo/waves/round-25/a2-build/out/ExecRawReplicate/K1/props.json)
                    = 064368843fe8eb5551e2741287e767aba6c265abf860ae00b16dc310c9e079f8
```

`git cat-file -p 3d9bd8d:.ufo/waves/round-25/a2-build/out/ExecRawReplicate/K1/props.json | sha256sum` prints the second value; the same file's own `out/ExecRawReplicate/summary.md:5` states it correctly, and all 33 digests in `summary.md` are blob-class. The advice table's third cell of the K1 row is 63 hex chars (`6939aec123597421a7c985c4…`), so it is not even a token under the frozen regex — the tail-regeneration pattern starts after 16 characters in every cell of that table. The file's own claim at `ExecRawReplicate.md:33` — "Stored sha256 values were re-verified against the files on disk (all match)" — is contradicted by the committed bytes for 22 of its digests.

This is exactly the defect class the capability was acquired to catch (round-38 S472: "first eight characters equal to the real one, hashes nothing"), reproduced at 16 characters, and it is **outside the counter's population by construction**. It is a finding, not something repaired here.

Secondary, smaller: the token regex is lowercase-only (`(?<![0-9a-f])[0-9a-f]{64}(?![0-9a-f])`), so uppercase digests are invisible. In the excluded population there is exactly 1 such run: `AAAA…` (64 A's) at `.ufo/waves/round-41/w1/out/R41A02/v2b-drift.sc.md:7`; the in-population tree has none. There are also 32 hex runs of length 62–66 or 150 in excluded docs that the regex cannot see (5 of length 63, 6 of 62, 17 of 65, 2 of 66, 2 of 150). The 40-char runs (12107 occurrences, 1186 distinct) are a separate length-excluded class, not a git-object population: only 7 of the first 40 distinct ones resolve with `git cat-file -t`.

## 7. Defect and proposed fix (not applied — this seat writes no lane files)

**Defect (operability, claim statement overstates the population):**
`SCORECARD.md:128` and `SCORECARD.md:2269` — "Find every full SHA-256 **the lane's documents** cite and classify it … exit 1 on any unexplained digest" — and `corpus/lessons/round-42.md:12` — "`tools/digest-ledger.py` finds every full SHA-256 **the lane's documents** cite" — state the universe without the `.ufo/` exclusion that the tool docstring (`tools/digest-ledger.py:14-15`) and the counter contract (`tools/round42-digest-audit.py:9-11`) both declare. **Proposed fix (one line per surface, no counter change needed):** append the population to the sentence in all three places, e.g.

> … cite (tracked `.md`/`.tsv` files outside `.ufo/`, minus `registry/digest-ledger.tsv`) and classify it … exit 1 on any unexplained digest in that population

Nothing in `tools/digest-ledger.py`, `tools/round42-digest-audit.py`, the preregistration or the receipts needs to change: the contract already says what the tool does; only the prose surfaces drop the qualifier. If the lane later wants the excluded population covered, the 22 `wrong_unflagged` digests already qualify for the frozen `wrong` ledger class (their `correct` value is a committed blob sharing ≥8 leading characters), but the rows would have no effect until `is_doc` (`tools/digest-ledger.py:59-60`) admits `.ufo/`, and the 924 `unknown` would still need `external`/`unverifiable` rows — so the honest move for round 42 is the wording fix plus routing the 22 as a defect.
