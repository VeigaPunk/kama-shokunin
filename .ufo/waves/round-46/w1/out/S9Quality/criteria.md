What the three rows add, and what they leave alone:

- **KQ66 is the input half of KQ43.** KQ43 binds a gate receipt's *outputs* to the
  revisions it evaluated; KQ66 binds a live/deploy receipt's *inputs* (model file,
  engine binary, probe script) to a URL and digest that a fresh session can
  re-fetch. KQ43's fields stay as they are.
- **KQ67 supplements KQ22 and KQ7, it does not supersede them.** KQ22 checks that
  no `corpus/SOURCES.md` *row* is rewritten inside one round (per-row,
  `tools/sources-append-only.py`); KQ67 checks the five files the lane treats as
  append-only at *file-body* level against the whole history
  (`tools/append-only-audit.py`). KQ7's append-at-end rule is untouched.
- **KQ68 tightens the round-44 release-recheck verdicts.** KQ58's `behind`-status
  rule and the round-44 ledger format stay; KQ68 requires the `holds-reworded` and
  `drifted` verdicts of any later ledger to carry both machine witnesses.
