Its `/health` answers "is the router process alive", and its `/ready` "has startup finished" — neither answers "is the thing at this baseURL the model this config believes it is". Quote support: PROVINCE Refuses, cross-lane-scope.md:28. Adding it would also stretch the one entry to a fourth product and bring a Chinese-language row as a new coverage claim (S443 "closes the lane's standing gap in Chinese evidence"), which is a separate claim from the frozen process-ownership one.
