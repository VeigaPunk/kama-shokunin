### F1 — MEDIUM — row 2 rule letter `A-corr` contradicts the file's own governing declaration (SCORECARD.md:6)
- Evidence: `git -C <lane> show 3a09a1d93dcc16799cf6c4b48786e6440af134c4:SCORECARD.md | sed -n 6p` → "**Current authority (round 46):** … The `## Axes` table, §Planned, §Retired, §Saturation and §Capabilities below are **re-derived from the tree** by `tools/round42-selfdesc-reconcile.py` … and kept current in rounds 43, 44, 45 and 46; the round-5 banner and the head sections they replace are kept verbatim under §Historical head sections."
- `A-corr` (per the sibling rows' notes: corrections land as new entries / appended, old text stays) asserts a file that only grows by appended corrections. SCORECARD.md's declared behavior is append-only for dated round sections plus **in-place re-derivation of the named head sections** every round. The contract's own note concedes this ("the head sections named at SCORECARD.md:6 are re-derived in place since round 42, so a head re-anchor is an expected partial"), so the truth lives only in the note while the rule letter says otherwise. The v2 rule vocabulary (A, A-row, A-corr) has no letter for this hybrid; the letter overstates.
- Impact: a reader of the contract alone concludes SCORECARD.md is corrections-appended-only; the lane's C1 lane leg will keep reporting head re-anchors as `partial` events (exit 1) exactly as the note predicts. Calibration-relevant wording defect in a frozen artifact.
- Fix (finding, not an in-place edit): a future contract revision should either reword the note to lead ("rule holds except the head sections declared re-derived-in-place at SCORECARD.md:6 — head re-anchors are expected partials") or extend the rule vocabulary (e.g. `A-corr-head`). Row text proposed (unapplied): keep `SCORECARD.md | A-corr | corpus/README.md:759 | dated round sections append-only; EXCEPT the head sections named at SCORECARD.md:6, which tools/round42-selfdesc-reconcile.py re-derives in place each round — a head re-anchor is an expected partial`.
