## 8. Impact on the claim / frontier

- The flip `met=False → met=True` is real (D-nothing control reproduced, independent seed), so the kill rule is satisfied on its face; score 8→8 means no inflation is at stake.
- What is not established: that the lane holds a capability that finds and *explains* cited digests. Under the frozen predicate a ledger that explains 4 of 10 (and lies about a 5th) passes, the correction of the round-38 wrong digest is optional, the tool may be the counter itself, and the two round-41 counters may be stubs. The SCORECARD rows at `SCORECARD.md:128` and `:2269` describe the tool's behaviour correctly ("exit 1 on any unexplained digest") but the row's first-working round 42 is earned on evidence weaker than the row's prose.
- For the frontier step: D1 is the interesting one — a `blob-member`/`#zcat` class makes the ledger auditable against committed bytes instead of host state, which is the only way this capability becomes tree-verifiable (D4's portability problem disappears with it). `considerthese/2026-10-25.md` proposals 3 and 4 remain unimplemented and are the natural next capability.
