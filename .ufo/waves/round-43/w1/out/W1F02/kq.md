## Notes

- **KQ54 needs no edit to `SKILL.md:1325` to be satisfiable.** Registering the 16
  counters makes `--selftest`'s lane arm pass, so the documented `pass=6 fail=0`
  becomes true; the criterion still requires the number to be re-derived from a run
  at the landed tip, so a future round that breaks arm 6 must restate the line.
- **KQ55 and KQ50 partition one population.** KQ50 owns exact 64-hex runs; KQ55 owns
  32–127 runs that are not 64. The split is at `tools/round43-hexrun-audit.py:18`
  (64 is excluded) and `tools/digest-ledger.py:67` (only 64 is admitted). Neither
  criterion may be reported as green while the other's population is uncounted;
  that is how the round-42 ledger passed with 73 unexplained runs beside it.
- **KQ56 is deliberately a refetch, not a hash.** The committed copy's SHA-256 is
  already covered by KQ50-style checks; the hole R42C06 opened is that a fabricated
  copy can be internally consistent. Only a live fetch of the named URL closes it,
  and the criterion says a failed fetch is a 0, so an offline round cannot claim it.
- **Boundary of this draft.** These rows judge counters and prose the round writes;
  they do not decide the axis score. C1/C2/C3 remain scored by their frozen counters
  and the non-author closure.
