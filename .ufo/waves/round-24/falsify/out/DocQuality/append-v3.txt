## Round24 continuation — KQ35 witness classes, no-trade anchor, withdrawn original, pairing bound (append-only)

This continuation adds no criterion and rewrites no row. KQ35 stands as written; the following fixes scope KQ35's own wording left open, and each item is answerable from committed bytes.

- **KQ35's paired obligations are discharged by a substantive class witness, per obligation and per class.** Complete-state obligation: a control cell that must carry a normal verdict — exit 0 or 1 — answering instead with a refusal, exit 2; that is the observable shape of a refusal metadata alone produced. Unavailable-state obligation: a control cell whose required object is genuinely unavailable, so the correct answer is a content-derived refusal, exit 2, answered instead with a valid verdict, exit 0 or 1; that is the observable shape of an unavailable object being accepted. A complete-state witness discharges no unavailable-state obligation, and the reverse.
- **A lost or altered pinned refusal reason is reason-loss sensitivity, never acceptance credit.** KQ34 requires the refusal reason to be pinned, and a mutant that drops or rewrites that reason is detected and reported. It shows the reason-pinning check fires; it does not show that an unavailable object was accepted, so it earns no acceptance credit and cannot stand as the unavailable-state witness above.
- **INVALID-EXPERIMENT and RUNTIME-FAULT outcomes earn no credit.** A traceback, an internal error, a timeout, a harness leak, or a failure outside the mutant's declared envelope is an INVALID-EXPERIMENT or RUNTIME-FAULT outcome: 0 credit for any obligation, reported separately from the class witnesses. In the V6/V7 enumeration such an outcome is a reason difference on the same could-not-evaluate exit as a content-derived refusal, so a lost named reason can still earn KQ34 reason-loss sensitivity — but never KQ35 acceptance credit.
- **A probe's exit 0 is a completed measurement, not an admission.** It records that a finite, named inventory was measured over a pinned population; it is not itself an admission of the round's claims. An obligation is discharged only by a substantive class witness on a named control of that class. Report each survivor with its reason; a survivor without a documented reason discharges no obligation.
- **The no-trade rule is unchanged and is anchored here.** `AXES.md` §Working the axes (fleet root, lines 25–38): a round is admitted only if every claim verifies **and no axis is harmed** — a trade anywhere is not an improvement. Disclosure of a trade does not waive the rule. A correct runnable workflow lost and excused as a disclosed trade is not an admissible round outcome.
- **The initial expectation is withdrawn, not achieved.** The initial registration's blanket unknown-citation expectation (`.ufo/waves/round-24/preregistration.json` `expected_after[3]`) is withdrawn and not achieved; it is retained as the original view and reported as withdrawn, never as satisfied. The prospective correction is `.ufo/waves/round-24/oracle-amendment-1.json`, and the 47-cell manifest `tests/report-audit/promisor-grid-expectations.json` (`d86b7bea…`) is the frozen graded authority and is not rewritten.
- **Pairing coverage is bounded, and the bound is stated rather than implied.** In the frozen 47-cell manifest, 42 of 47 cells participate in a plain-twin pair; five `policy` cells carry no twin inside the frozen 47 — PG21, PG43, PG46 and PG47 (exit 2) and PG48 (exit 0). Those cells are graded by their own per-cell exit, kinds, defects and reason class, not by a twin verdict equality. Private plain metadata-only companions for those cells are planned outside the frozen 47; if and when they run they are expected to report EXITPAIR(2,2) for each of PG21, PG43, PG46 and PG47 — both sides refusing, with no verdict-document equality available, because a refusal emits no verdict document — and EXITPAIR(0,0) for PG48, both sides clean. That is an expectation, not a measurement. The companions add no primary score, change no manifest cell and no expectation byte, and do not convert the frozen 47 into a paired population.
