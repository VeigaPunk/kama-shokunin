### F9 — "every backticked .ufo path cited by the round-40 and round-41 SCORECARD sections": global exemption, heading-regex population, silent token filter

Three separate weakenings at `:229-251`:

1. **Global exemption.** `exempt` accumulates the bodies of *all* record-correction sections (`:240-241`) and `:247` tests `tok not in exempt` — a substring test over the concatenation. One quotation of a token in any correction section exempts that token in every round-40/41 section. Control and counterexample:
   ```
   G1 (missing token, no quotation):   receipts=1  mismatches=1 met=False  EXIT=1
       detail: receipts: sections of rounds (40, 41) cite missing .ufo/waves/round-41/missing-receipt.md

                                        receipts=0  mismatches=0 met=True   EXIT=0
   ```
2. **Population by heading regex.** `:242` selects sections whose heading matches `Round[ -]?40|41`. At the measured commit this swept `### Historical axes table (round-3 assessment; superseded in round 41)` into the population (6 headings, 9 paths). Conversely, a round-40/41 section whose heading does not spell a round (e.g. "## Digest corrections") is outside the population the predicate says is covered.
3. **Silent token filter.** `:247` skips tokens containing `<`, `>`, `*`, `$` or a space; `:248` expands `{a,b}` braces. The predicate says "every backticked .ufo path"; a real missing path written with a glob is skipped without a note.

Smallest safe correction: build `exempt` per section (exempt a token only for the section that quotes it), match section membership by position (the round-N section spans) rather than by heading text, and count skipped tokens in a `skipped` counter that the summary line prints.
